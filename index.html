University Learning Plan

01

Introduction

Unlike secondary school, university requires me to make more independent decisions about what to learn and how to manage my time. As a first-year student at CUHK, I believe university education offers opportunities for growth far beyond academic achievement. CUHK provides an environment where I can deepen my understanding of the world and develop into a more mature and responsible individual. Guided by the SMART principle, I have set three goals to guide my academic and personal development throughout my university studies.

02

Goal 1: Becoming an Independent Learner with Intellectual Curiosity

I’m interested in financial markets and aim to apply my mathematical skills to conduct quantitative analysis. At CUHK, I will work hard to establish a solid foundation in mathematics, statistics and computer science, which are essential to my academic interests and major.

To be more specific, my academic goal is not merely to achieve a high GPA. I have made a list of courses I plan to take online. Most of them are offered by top universities in the world such as MIT, UC Berkeley and Stanford. Based on my planned schedule, I will devote at least 600 hours to independent learning during my first year at CUHK, so I will have sufficient time to prepare for my research, competitions and internships in the next three years.

Here is the self-study program I designed for myself.

Although I have a solid foundation in mathematics, I have much less experience in computer science. I have set a goal of spending 10–20 hours a week on AI-assisted coding (not included in independent learning time), so that I can keep up with developments in AI and deepen my understanding of AI and computer science. It can also help boost my efficiency while studying or researching.

These goals will be challenging to achieve. Throughout my university studies, I will develop my own study method, which will integrate AI tools and require self-discipline.

Here is the homepage of a project called Study OS, which I developed with AI assistance and can help me sort my documents and automatically generate notes and practice examinations.

Targets
600 hours Independent learning in the first year
10–20 hours AI-assisted coding each week
03

Goal 2: Developing My Identity and Sense of Responsibility

Entering university gives me greater freedom to make my own decisions, but also requires me to take greater responsibility. Through interacting with people from different backgrounds and contributing to CUHK, I aim to build a stronger sense of belonging to the university.

To achieve this goal, I plan to find like-minded people and establish a student organization or society, where we can develop teamwork and communication skills through collaborative projects and the exchange of ideas. I hope to complete a research project or achieve a specific result in a competition, for example, a quant competition like IQC, before graduation. I will take responsibility for organizing at least one activity and seek feedback from other members, since this will help me evaluate my teamwork and leadership skills. To begin with, I plan to form a study group before May 2027, the end of my first year at CUHK. I will also actively participate in activities organized by my college or school. Through these experiences, I hope to become an active and responsible member of the CUHK community.

Targets
May 2027 Study group formed by the end of year one
At least 1 Activity organized, feedback collected
04

Goal 3: Achieving Balanced Personal Development

University learning should extend beyond academic study. Besides enhancing skills related to my academic goals, I’m also interested in social sciences and humanities, such as history and politics. These subjects can broaden my perspectives and enrich my university experience by deepening my understanding of society. To further explore these interests, I plan to read at least one book on history or politics each semester. After finishing each book, I will write a short reflection on what I have learned and how it has influenced my understanding of society.

Maintaining a healthy and balanced lifestyle is also important. As I plan to spend considerable time on independent learning, managing my time and energy will be particularly important. I plan to walk at least 6000 steps on weekdays and 4000 steps at weekends. Every day while taking a shower, I will review my daily schedule and reflect on my physical and mental well-being. I aim for around 7.5 hours of sleep on most nights to keep me healthy and efficient during the day. By May 2027, I hope to have developed sustainable habits that allow me to balance academic achievement with personal well-being.

Targets
6000 / 4000 Steps on weekdays / weekends
7.5 hours Sleep on most nights
05

Challenges, Monitoring and Adjustment

Balancing my academic goals, independent learning and extracurricular activities will be challenging. To manage my time effectively, I will review my progress every week and adjust my schedule when necessary. If I fall behind my learning targets for two consecutive weeks, I will prioritize essential coursework and reduce less important commitments. At the end of each semester, I will evaluate my achievements and revise my plan to ensure that my goals remain realistic and achievable.

06

Conclusion: Becoming a Better Version of Myself

By achieving these three goals, I hope to become not only a more capable learner, but also a responsible and well-rounded individual. I believe university education is a continuous process of learning, reflection and personal growth. This plan will serve as a foundation for my development throughout my university years.
