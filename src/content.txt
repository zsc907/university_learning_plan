University Learning Plan
Introduction
As a first-year student at CUHK, I have already noticed that university calls for more independent decisions than secondary school — about what to study, and about how I spend my hours. That freedom is what I want to make the most of, not grades alone. Chung Chi College's motto, "In Pursuit of Excellence", points me the same way: keep improving, in my studies and in myself. At CUHK and Chung Chi I have a place where I can come to understand the world more deeply, and become a more mature and responsible person. Guided by the SMART principle, I have set three goals for my academic and personal development across my university years.
Goal 1: Becoming an Independent Learner with Intellectual Curiosity
I’m interested in financial markets and aim to apply my mathematical skills to conduct quantitative analysis. At CUHK, I will work hard to establish a solid foundation in mathematics, statistics and computer science, which are essential to my academic interests and major. 
To be more specific, my academic goal is not merely to achieve a high GPA. I have made a list of courses I plan to take online. Most of them are offered by top universities in the world such as MIT, UC Berkeley and Stanford. Based on my planned schedule, I will devote at least 600 hours to independent learning during my first year at CUHK, so I will have sufficient time to prepare for my research, competitions and internships in the next three years.

Although I have a solid foundation in mathematics, I have much less experience in computer science. I have set a goal of spending 10–20 hours a week on AI-assisted coding (not included in independent learning time), so that I can keep up with developments in AI and deepen my understanding of AI and computer science. It can also help boost my efficiency while studying or researching.
These goals will be challenging to achieve. Throughout my university studies, I will develop my own study method, which will integrate AI tools and require self-discipline.

Goal 2: Developing My Identity and Sense of Responsibility
Entering university gives me greater freedom to make my own decisions, but also requires me to take greater responsibility. I hope to develop a stronger sense of belonging to CUHK by interacting with people from different backgrounds and contributing to the university community.
As a first step, I plan to form a study group before May 2027, the end of my first year at CUHK. Building on this, I hope to bring together like-minded students and establish a student organization or society where we can exchange ideas, collaborate on projects and develop our teamwork and communication skills. Before graduation, I aim to complete a research project or achieve a specific result in a quantitative competition such as IQC. I also intend to take responsibility for organizing at least one activity and gather feedback from other members to evaluate my teamwork and leadership skills.
Beyond these academic initiatives, I will participate in activities organized by Chung Chi College and seek opportunities to contribute to the College community. Through both collaborative projects and participation in college activities, I believe I can gradually develop a genuine sense of belonging to CUHK.
Goal 3: Achieving Balanced Personal Development
Inspired by Chung Chi College's emphasis on whole-person development, I believe university learning should extend beyond academic study. Besides enhancing skills related to my academic goals, I’m also interested in social sciences and humanities, such as history and politics. These subjects can broaden my perspectives and enrich my university experience by deepening my understanding of society. To further explore these interests, I plan to read at least one book on history or politics each semester. After finishing each book, I will write a short reflection on what I have learned and how it has influenced my understanding of society.
As I intend to devote considerable time to independent learning, I need to pay attention to how I manage my time and energy without neglecting my health. To maintain an active lifestyle, I plan to walk at least 6,000 steps on weekdays and 4,000 steps at weekends. I also aim to get around 7.5 hours of sleep on most nights so that I can stay healthy and focused during the day. Besides these routines, I will use the time I spend taking a shower each day to review my schedule and reflect on my physical and mental well-being. By May 2027, I hope these practices will have become part of my daily life, helping me maintain a balance between academic achievement and personal well-being.
Challenges, Monitoring and Adjustment
Balancing my academic goals, independent learning and extracurricular activities will be challenging. To manage my time effectively, I will review my progress every week and adjust my schedule when necessary. If I fall behind my learning targets for two consecutive weeks, I will prioritize essential coursework and reduce less important commitments. At the end of each semester, I will evaluate my achievements and revise my plan to ensure that my goals remain realistic and achievable.
Conclusion: Becoming a Better Version of Myself
If I do achieve these three goals, I hope to become a stronger learner, and a more responsible and well-rounded person as well. University education, to my mind, is a continuous process of learning, reflection and personal growth. Throughout my years here, this plan will be the foundation my development is built on.
